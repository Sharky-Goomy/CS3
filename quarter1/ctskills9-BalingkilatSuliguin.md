# Scenario: PSHS School Canteen
# Step 1 : Identifying The Big Problem
Main Problem: The small size of the PSHS canteen makes it prone to frequent crowding and delays during student's lunch breaks. These are mainly caused due to:
- Inability of the students to decide on what to order, despite being in the front of the line.
- Manual calculations of the total price of items, and their respective changes from the cashiers.
- Lack of real time inventory tracking on items that are running out of stock.
# Step 2: Identifying Three to Four Sub-Problems
1. Ordering Inefficiency: Students in the front of the line takes too much time to decide on what to order.
2. Manual Calculations: Cashiers have to calculate the total item price and their respective changes manually, which takes some additional time.
3. Deficit on Inventory Tracking: There is no system that tracks if a certain item is running out or is officially out of stock.
4. Queue Congestion: The line where students go through lacks a pre-ordering mechanism to speed up the process.
